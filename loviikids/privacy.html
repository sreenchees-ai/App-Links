Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · App ID: com.fun.loviikids

The short version for parents

  • LoVii Kids is made for children aged 3 to 8. There are no accounts, names, photos, location or chat.
  • The microphone is used only while your child plays the voice game, and only after you allow it. We never record or keep voice audio.
  • Stars, levels and settings stay on your device. We don't collect them.
  • Free players see ads from Google AdMob. They are set for children and are not personalised. Premium has no ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see card or bank details.
  • Purchases, rewarded ads and outside links sit behind a grown-up question.

1. Who we are

LoVii Kids: Fun and Learn ("LoVii Kids", "the app", "we") is developed by Abrar Ali (rebrar.in), Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India. This policy explains what happens to information when someone uses the LoVii Kids Android app or its web version.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store a child's or parent's name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, precise location or any other information that identifies a person. The app has no sign-up, no profiles, no chat and no way for children to share anything with other people.

3. The microphone and voice play

In LoVii Kids, a child says the name of the friend at the glass (for example "carrot") to bring it through. To make this work:

  • The app asks for microphone permission only when voice play is started, never at launch. You can say no and turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • The microphone is on only while the game is listening. It switches off when the app goes into the background or the child leaves the game.
  • Speech is turned into words by the speech service built into your phone. On most Android phones this is Google's speech service, which may send the audio to Google's servers to convert it into text. That processing is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • LoVii Kids receives only the recognised words, uses them at once to check the answer, and then discards them. We do not record, save or upload voice audio or transcripts, and they are never shared with advertisers.
  • Spoken praise ("Carrot! Wonderful!") is made by your phone's text-to-speech service on the device.

In the web version, speech recognition is provided by your browser (in Google Chrome, by Google).

4. Information kept on your device

To remember progress, the app stores a small amount of information on the device only:

WhatWhy
Stars, levels, badges, worlds visitedTo show progress and rewards
Settings (sound, volume, speech accent, graphics, show names)To keep the grown-up's choices
Premium status and worlds opened for a dayTo unlock what was bought or earned

This information is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, it may be included in your own Google device backup. You can delete it with Reset progress in the grown-up settings (lock button) or by uninstalling the app.

5. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ad network. We have set every ad request as follows:

  • marked as child-directed (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as under the age of consent (for the EU GDPR and similar laws);
  • non-personalised: no interest-based ads, no remarketing and no profiling;
  • limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences);
  • the app does not use the Android advertising ID.

Ads appear as a banner at the bottom of the home, worlds and rewards screens (never over the game), as a full-screen ad after every two finished levels, and as an optional video a grown-up can choose to watch to open a locked world for a day. To deliver and count ads and prevent fraud, Google may process technical information such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system and app version, as limited for child-directed requests. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Players with Premium see no ads.

6. Purchases

LoVii Kids Premium is offered as a monthly subscription and as a one-time lifetime purchase, both sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never receive card, UPI or bank details. Google Play shares a purchase record with the app (product, purchase token, and whether it is active) so the app can unlock Premium and restore it on a new phone. The app checks this record with Google. If we run a purchase-check server, it receives only the app ID, product ID and purchase token, asks Google whether the purchase is valid, and does not keep any personal information.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

7. Protections for children

  • LoVii Kids is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • Buying, rewarded ads, the Premium page and links that leave the app are behind a grown-up question. Google Play also asks for its own purchase confirmation.
  • There are no social features, no in-app messaging and no links to social media.

8. Internet use

The game itself works offline. An internet connection is used for speech recognition (on most phones), ads, and Google Play purchases.

9. Sharing

We do not sell or rent information. The only third parties involved are Google (speech recognition on most phones, AdMob ads and Google Play billing), as described above.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off the microphone at any time: phone Settings › Apps › LoVii Kids › Permissions.
  • Delete game progress with Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Avoid ads completely with Premium.
  • Parents and guardians can contact us with any question or request, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, COPPA or the GDPR. Since we don't hold personal information about you or your child, there is usually nothing on our side to access or delete.

11. Security

The app loads its game files from inside the app, blocks connections to other websites, and only talks to Google's services for speech, ads and purchases.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how information is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes on Google Play.
